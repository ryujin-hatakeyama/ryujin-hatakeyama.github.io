I saw a friend for the first time in a while today, and said that there might be something rather good, in its own way, about eventually reaching a point where neither of us was particularly interested in what the other was making. At the same time, though, as long as we each have bodies of our own, however far that tendency developed, I do not think it could ever be fully realized.
